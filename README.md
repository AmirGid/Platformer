# Demonic Flame

A browser-based HTML5 canvas platformer. Move through a moonlit forest overlooking a city, fight off imps, and manage your life flask as you push east through connected stages.

## Stages

Stages are linked metroidvania style: walk off an edge or drop down a shaft to reach the next area. Imps you've killed stay dead when you return.

```
 Shadow Cliffs (J) = Ruined Cathedral (K, finish)
       | climb up
 Haunted Graves (H)   <- reached only through the Frozen Den's secret portal

 Moonlit Ridge = Ember Hollow = Ashen Woods = Cinder Cliffs
                      | shaft                     | shaft
 Frozen Den     = Molten Core /              The Chute
                  Frozen Spire                    | shaft
                                             Obsidian Gate (Frost Wyrm)
```

The route: Ember Hollow, down into the golem's room, beat it, climb the Frozen Spire back up into Ember Hollow, then east through Ashen Woods (needs the double jump) and Cinder Cliffs, down The Chute to the Obsidian Gate, where the Frost Wyrm's heart gives you the dash. Climb back out, return to the Frozen Den and double-jump and dash up to its secret ledge: the portal there throws you into the shadow realm, up through Haunted Graves and Shadow Cliffs to the Ruined Cathedral.

0. **Burning Village** (prologue, where every run starts): the hero's home village, burning under a violet sky. A long, quiet walk east down its cobbled street: no enemies, no pits and no ledges, only the skeletons of the villagers who didn't make it, scattered along the street. At the east end a swirling vortex opens as you come near: walk into it and it pulls you through to Moonlit Ridge, where it hangs behind you a moment and then shrinks away to nothing (one way: there's no walking back).
1. **Moonlit Ridge**: solid ground and scattered rock ledges. You arrive here through the village's vortex. Exit east to Ember Hollow.
2. **Ember Hollow**: lava pits in the ground (falling in costs one flask hit and sends you back to the stage entrance), a high staircase route and more, faster imps. The last pit is a dark shaft instead of lava: drop into it to reach the Molten Core. Exit east to Ashen Woods.
3. **Molten Core**: a volcanic cavern arena with the **Magma Golem** boss (14 hits). It throws fireballs (swat them with your attack), leaps at you and sends ground shockwaves (jump them), and breathes fire. Below half health it speeds up and throws three fireballs at once. Beating it shatters the golem, flings out its still-pumping **heart** (grab it to fill your flask and unlock the **double jump**: press jump again in mid-air; once the frost settles a card with a looping demo shows how, and stays up until you've tried it) and a wave of frost turns the cavern into ice for good. In fire form you take no damage and touching the golem hurts it.
4. **Frozen Spire**: the frozen cavern becomes the bottom of a tall ice shaft. Climb about 40 ice ledges (you can jump up through them from below) past winged imps that hover over the ledges, fly between them and swoop at you when you get close. Jump up from the glowing summit to climb back out into Ember Hollow. Its west side opens into the Smoldering Den (the east side, E on the map, is not built yet).
5. **Ashen Woods**: grey forest east of Ember Hollow. Its last lava gap is too wide for one jump: you need the golem's double jump to reach Cinder Cliffs.
6. **Cinder Cliffs**: red-lit cliffs with winged imps; the last pit is a shaft down into The Chute. A gnarled old tree closes off the east end: you stop at its trunk (it's trimmed back so nothing hangs over the shaft). Two ledges climb up beside it: from the top one, double-jump over the top of the trunk to find Agam's Squishy Forest.
7. **The Chute**: a narrow, tall shaft. Work your way down the ledges past winged imps; the pit at the bottom drops into the Obsidian Gate. Double-jump up out of the top to go back to Cinder Cliffs.
8. **Obsidian Gate**: frozen wastes with frost golems and wisps, then the **Frost Wyrm**. When it wakes, a wall of ice rises out of the last pit behind you so you can't run; once the wyrm is dead the wall cracks apart (the same crack frames as the Frozen Den's wall) and melts away. When the wyrm bursts apart it flings out its still-beating **frozen heart**: touch it to fill your flask and unlock the **dash** (a card with a looping demo shows how, and stays up until you've dashed twice): press **Shift** (or **C**, or tap the DASH button) for a fast, flat burst the way you're facing. You pass straight through enemies and their attacks while it lasts; on the ground it recharges in about half a second, and in the air you get one dash per jump. A high ice ledge near the start, under a beam of light, leads back: jump up off it to climb into The Chute.
9. **Frozen Den**: an icy side cave west of the golem's room, closed off on its west side by a wall of icicled cliff. Its lower part is already cracked, a hint that it can be broken: each sword hit cracks it further, and after the third it shatters, pauses a moment and fades away, opening the way west into Itay's Secret Lab. Climb its ice ledges to the top left to find the **world map**: once you have it, press **M** (or tap the map button) to open a map of the stages you've visited, with a marker showing where you are. The game pauses while it's open.
10. **Haunted Graves** (H): you step out of the portal into a violet graveyard of floating islands over a bottomless void. At the far end the islands climb to a beam of light: jump up out of the top into Shadow Cliffs. Its enemies are **scythe wraiths** (hooded reapers that float over their patch of ground, raise their scythe as a warning, then glide forward and swing) and **wailing ghosts** (they drift between spots, scream, then spit a cursed soul you can swat away); both take two hits. The shadow realm sits far off in the top-right corner of the world map, cut off from the rest of the world.
11. **Shadow Cliffs** (J): you pop up out of a shaft (drop back down it to return to the graves). Cross broken cliffs over the void, with a high road above, and exit east. Scythe wraiths and wailing ghosts guard the way here too.
12. **Ruined Cathedral** (K, finish): the **Wraith Knight** (14 hits). It raises its burning sword and lunges (only the sword hurts: jump it or back off, then hit it while it hangs still), gathers lost souls and flings them at you (swat them out of the air), and blinks out to reappear behind you. You can walk through its ghostly body. Once it's destroyed, the ending cinematic plays (skip with the SKIP button, Esc, Enter or Space), then the violet glow lifts (the ruins fade to plain moonlight), the curse on the hero breaks (dark wisps swirl in, a white flash, and he's a young man again) and the portal behind it opens: step through to win.
- **Agam's Squishy Forest** (secret): past Cinder Cliffs' east end, over the top of the old tree. A pastel wood of cushion paths, candy trees and poodles, with floating cushion islands to jump up through. Walk in and the **Squishy Queen** (20 hits) takes you on, her pink poodle beside her. She walks you down and picks from three moves: she throws a bouncy rainbow heart at you (swat it, jump or dash past it; three in a fan below half health); she crouches, leaps high and lands on you with a squish that sends a heart rolling out each way along the ground (jump them, then hit her while she catches her breath); or she clasps her hands and her **Squishy Shield**, a rainbow bubble, wraps her: hits bounce off and bounce you back, so wait it out. Below half health she's quicker and glows pink. Beaten, she sits down in a heap, then smiles and waves. Her fight plays **Forest Boss Battle**. Walk west to go back (you arrive on the top ledge beside the tree).
- **Itay's Secret Lab** (secret): behind the Frozen Den's ice wall, reached by breaking the wall's lower part and walking west: a machine works buried in the cave, with a steel deck floor and three floating decks (two low, one high between them) that you can jump up through from below. Walk in and the lab's east door slides shut behind you (you can't leave until he's beaten; it slides open again once he's gone), and **Itay** takes you on: the hardest fight in the game (30 hits). A kid in a powered battle suit, he walks you down and picks from four moves: a palm blaster (the palm glows, then a stream of plasma bolts: swat, jump or dash through them); an energy shield (hits bounce off, and he answers each with a punch and a fan of bolts: wait it out); a jet slam (he crouches, rockets up over you, dives and sends shockwaves both ways: jump them, then hit him while he gets up); and a flying barrage (jet boots on, he rains bolts down from above: climb the decks to reach him). Below half health his suit runs hot: everything is faster, the blaster fires five bolts, the slam comes twice and the barrage fires in pairs. Touching him hurts too. Beaten, he drops to one knee, his suit sparking, and teleports away, leaving his clockwork **heart** behind: it clanks down, rolls across the floor and settles there beating with an electric glow. Touch it to fill your flask and gain the **force field** (a card with a looping demo shows how, and stays up until the field has gone up once): from then on every third kill throws a crackling violet force field around you for 5 seconds (three pips under the flask count your kills toward the next one). Anything that touches the field gets a purple shock that costs it one hit (an imp dies, a frost golem cracks, a boss loses a hit), then a second's grace before it can be shocked again, flickering purple and white the whole time. Kills made by the field count toward the next one too. His fight has its own track, **Machine Pulse** (`itay_music.mp3`): it starts when he comes into view and hands back to the main theme once he's beaten. Walk east to go back.

**Max-life flasks**: two small corked bottles are hidden on secret ledges. Each adds a hit to your life flask (it stretches taller) and fills it. One sits at the top right of the very first screen and needs the double jump; the other is at the top right of the Frozen Den and needs a double jump off the highest right-hand ledge followed by a mid-air dash (fire form's speed doesn't carry into the air, so it can't stand in for the dash). The portal into the shadow realm stands on that same ledge, right beside it.

Stages are defined in the `STAGES` list in `index.html`; add another entry to add a stage.


## Sound

Background music, the evil laugh, the cracking ice of the Frozen Den's breakable wall and the secret lab's metal door are recorded files. The laugh plays only after you come through a portal: when the village vortex closes behind you, and when each realm's arrival portal bursts. The main theme (`bgmusic.mp3`) plays on the title screen and in most stages. The Burning Village has its own quieter track (`village_music.mp3`), which hands over to the main theme once you step through the vortex. The Magma Golem has its own track, **Volcanous Fury** (`golem_music.mp3`): it starts as soon as the golem is on screen and hands back to the main theme the moment it dies. The Frost Wyrm does the same with its own track (`wyrm_music.mp3`), and so does Itay in the secret lab with **Machine Pulse** (`itay_music.mp3`), and the Squishy Queen with **Forest Boss Battle** (`queen_music.mp3`). The Wraith Knight has his own track, **Final Stand** (`boss_music.mp3`): it takes over the moment the knight comes into view, pauses with the game, and hands back to the main theme when you leave the cathedral or the ending plays. All other sound effects are synthesized in the browser (Web Audio API), so they need no files: sword swings, imp deaths, getting hurt, lava, jumps and the icy double jump, fire form, stage changes, the golem's roar, fireballs, leap, ground slam, flame breath, stone-crack hits, death and explosion, the heart's splat and distance-based heartbeat, the frost wave, the ability chime, the winged imps' screech, game over and the victory fanfare.

The golem's roar and death can use recorded files instead: set their paths in `SFX_FILES` in `index.html` and they replace the synthesized versions once loaded.

Press **N** or tap the speaker button to mute; the choice is remembered on that device. Sound starts after the first tap or key press (browsers require a user gesture).

Press **P** or **Esc** (or tap the pause button) to pause. The pause menu has music and sound-effect volume sliders, a vibration switch (Android phones; iPhones don't support web vibration) and a restart button. The game also pauses itself when you switch away from it.

## Running it

No build step — it's a static page. Either:

- Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or
- Serve the folder locally (recommended, avoids any `file://` quirks):

  ```bash
  python3 -m http.server 8000
  ```

  then visit `http://localhost:8000/index.html`.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys / A, D** — move left and right
- **Arrow Up** — jump
- **Space** — attack
- **X** — once you've defeated 4+ imps, ignite into the fire-elemental form for 8 seconds (runs much faster on the ground, though in mid-air you move at normal speed unless you dash; simply touching an enemy kills it instantly with no damage taken). A flame bar over your head burns down as it goes; in the last 2 seconds it turns red and blinks, you flicker and it sizzles, faster in the final second. When the timer runs out you revert to normal and the imp counter resets to 0.
- **R** — restart after game over or a win
- **F** (or the corner button at the top right) — full screen on a computer: just the game fills the screen, keeping its shape with black bars if needed; F or Esc goes back. Phones go full screen by themselves when the game starts.
- **Phones / tablets** — on-screen buttons appear automatically on touch screens

## Testing shortcuts (remove before going live)

These are for testing only. **Before the final release, remove both** (they're marked `TESTING ONLY` in `index.html`):

- **Stage links**: add `?stage=` to the address to start in any stage, with the abilities, flasks and beaten bosses you'd have by then. Use the stage number from the in-game banner (`?stage=0` for the Burning Village, `?stage=1` to `?stage=9`), `H`, `J` or `K`, or part of the name (`?stage=den`, `?stage=cathedral`). Add `&god` to switch on god mode for that visit, e.g. `index.html?stage=K&god`.
- **God mode**: the GOD button (or the G key) makes you impossible to hurt.
